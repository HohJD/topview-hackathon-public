01 The brief

Malaysia, stories that unite

Celebrate Malaysia's diversity, culture and shared spirit through an original AI generated festive film for a brand of your choice. It lands in the Merdeka and Malaysia Day season, so the films that work will be the ones that feel like home: unity, culture and national pride, told honestly.

  1. 01

    Form a team and pick a brand

    Two to four people. Any brand you like.

  2. 02

    Make the film with Topview

    An original Malaysia themed festive film, built on the day.

  3. 03

    Submit one final cut

    Between one and three minutes, uploaded by 16:00.

  4. 04

    Make it ring true

    Malaysian spirit, inclusivity, and authentic storytelling.

Rule

The Jalur Gemilang

If the flag features in your film, it must be portrayed respectfully and in accordance with Malaysian flag etiquette.

Rule

The 3R matters

Do not include or comment on race, religion and royalty, in accordance with Malaysian law.

07 Questions

Everything else

What exactly am I making?

A one to three minute festive brand film on the theme Malaysia, Stories That Unite. Pick any brand, then tell a Malaysia Day story through it.

Can I come without a team?

Come anyway. Teams are two to four people, and the check-in session exists to pair up people who arrive alone.

Do I need to know these tools already?

No. That is what the morning workshops are for.

Do I have to use Topview?

Your team gets credits and the stack is open all day, so you are strongly encouraged to. Bring other tools alongside it if you want.

Can I turn up with work already done?

No footage, edits, or renders made before 10:45. Ideas, scripts, and storyboards are fine, and you should arrive with a plan.

Who owns what we make?

You do. Entering lets us show your film in event recaps and on social. Nothing more.
