01 The brief

Malaysia, Stories That Unite

Create an original AI-generated festive film inspired by the spirit of Merdeka and Malaysia Day. Set during this national season of reflection and celebration, your film should feel authentically Malaysian: thoughtful, inclusive, distinctive, and made with respect for the many communities that call it home.

  1. 01

    Form a team

    Teams can have two to four people. Come with a team, or join one during team formation.

  2. 02

    Create your film with Topview

    All production must take place during the event, after the workshop ends at 10:45am.

  3. 03

    Submit one final cut

    Your completed film should be between one and two minutes long and submitted by 4:00pm.

  4. 04

    Keep it recognisably Malaysian

    Aim for representation that is respectful, inclusive, and grounded in the spirit of Malaysia Day.

  • Portray the Jalur Gemilang respectfully and in line with Malaysian flag etiquette.
  • Avoid sensitive 3R content relating to race, religion, and royalty.
  • Follow applicable Malaysian laws and community standards.

02 Made with Topview

A taste of what Topview can do

A few things made with Topview and Seedance 2.5. You will learn the same tools in the morning workshop, then have until 4:00pm to make something of your own.

03 Morning workshop

Learn the tools, then bring your idea to life

No prior experience with Topview or Seedance is needed. The workshop is designed to help first-time users get from a blank project to a finished video.

9:30am – 10:45am  ·  75 minutes

Topview & Seedance 2.5

You will learn how to:

  • Build a video from concept to final cut in Topview
  • Generate, edit, and assemble scenes efficiently
  • Use your credits thoughtfully while iterating quickly
  • Prompt Seedance 2.5 for movement and visuals that read well on screen
  • Maintain consistent characters and visual direction across scenes
  • Recognise common generation issues and work around them creatively

06 How your film will be judged

50 points across five criteria

Each category is worth 10 points. Judging is based on the submitted final cut.

  • 10 pts

    Storytelling & Narrative

    Is the story clear, original, and emotionally engaging?

  • 10 pts

    Creative Direction & Craft

    Are the visuals, pacing, editing, and use of AI purposeful and well executed?

  • 10 pts

    Series Potential

    Does the film introduce a world, idea, or set of characters compelling enough to make audiences want a second episode?

  • 10 pts

    Malaysia Relevance & Authenticity

    Does the film feel respectful, inclusive, and recognisably Malaysian?

  • 10 pts

    Hook & Engagement

    Does it capture attention early and leave a lasting impression?

08 FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What exactly am I making?

An original, AI-generated festive film inspired by the theme: Malaysia, Stories That Unite.

Can I come without a team?

Yes. Team formation takes place during check-in from 8:45am.

Do I need prior experience with these tools?

No. The morning workshop is designed for beginners as well as experienced creators.

Do I have to use Topview?

Yes. Topview is the official creation platform for the hackathon.

Can I bring work made before the event?

No. All work submitted for the competition must be created during the event, beginning after 10:45am.

Who owns the work we create?

You do. Participants keep full ownership of the films they create, and all usage rights stay with the team.

How many places are available?

Places are limited. Register early to secure your spot.
