The brief

Make a video that could not have been made a year ago.

That is the whole brief. No theme, no category, no required format. A 30 second spot, a music video, a fake trailer, a tool that generates video on demand: if it plays and it is yours, it counts.

You get two workshops before the sprint starts, one on the TopView stack and one on Seedance 2.5, so nobody spends their build time reading docs. After 10:45 the room is yours until submissions close at 16:00.

  • Hand in

    One finished video

    Up to three minutes, any aspect ratio, delivered as a file by 16:00. It has to play without you standing next to it explaining what it would have been.

  • Hand in

    A one page build note

    What you used, what you automated, what you did by hand. Judges read this alongside the video. Honesty scores better than mystique.

  • On stage

    Finalist demos at 17:00

    Judges shortlist between 16:00 and 17:00. Finalists play their video and take questions, then winners are announced at 18:00.

Run of day

Learn for two hours. Build for four and a half.

The morning gets you fluent in the tools. The rest of the day is a protected sprint, split by one real lunch, with a hard submission cutoff at 16:00 so demos start on time.

How it is judged

Four things, weighted.

Judges see the video first and read the build note second. Nothing is scored on how hard the day was for you, only on what showed up on screen.

TBC Panel to be confirmed. Proposal assumes three judges: one from TopView AI, one from AWS, one working filmmaker or creative director.

  • 30%

    Craft

    Does it hold together as a piece of video. Pacing, sound, cuts, and whether the shots belong in the same film.

  • 30%

    Pipeline

    How much of the work the machine actually did, and how cleverly. Ambition counts more than polish here.

  • 25%

    Idea

    Is it worth watching twice. A strong idea rendered roughly beats a beautiful thing with nothing in it.

  • 15%

    Finish

    Delivered on time, plays clean, correct format. Free points, and teams lose them every year.

Questions

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Either compete solo, or use the 45 minute team forming session at the start of the day. Plenty of people arrive alone.

Do I have to use TopView AI?

Your team gets credits and the stack is open all day, so you are strongly encouraged to. You can bring other tools in alongside it. The pipeline score rewards what you built, not which logo is on it.

Do I need to know Seedance or TopView already?

No. That is what the morning is for. The TopView workshop and the Seedance 2.5 tutorial both run before the sprint starts, and they are built for people opening the tools for the first time.

Can I turn up with work already done?

No footage, no edits, no renders made before 10:45. Ideas, scripts, storyboards, and pre-built scaffolding code are all fine, and you should absolutely arrive with a plan.

Who owns what we make?

You do. Entering grants TopView AI and AWS permission to show your video in event recaps and on social. Nothing more than that.

A laptop that can handle an edit, headphones, and a charger. If you work with a specific mouse, tablet, or audio interface, bring it.
