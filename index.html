The brief

Build a brand film that makes people feel something.

A brand film is a one to three minute advertisement that sells on emotion, not features. Think of the ads you still remember.

Pick any product, service, or cause. Real or invented, yours or someone else's. What matters is whether the room feels it.

  • Deliver

    The film

    One to three minutes, any aspect ratio, uploaded by 16:00.

  • Deliver

    A one page build note

    What you used, what you automated, what you did by hand.

  • On stage

    Finalist demos

    Judges shortlist from 16:00. Finalists play at 17:00 and take questions.

Questions

What is a brand film?

A short advertisement that sells on feeling rather than features. One to three minutes. Any product, service, or cause, real or invented.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Compete solo, or find a team at check-in. Plenty of people arrive alone.

Do I need to know these tools already?

No. That is what the morning workshops are for.

Do I have to use TopView?

Your team gets credits and the stack is open all day, so you are strongly encouraged to. Bring other tools alongside it if you want.

Can I turn up with work already done?

No footage, edits, or renders made before 10:45. Ideas, scripts, and storyboards are fine, and you should arrive with a plan.

Who owns what we make?

You do. Entering lets us show your film in event recaps and on social. Nothing more.
